Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about keratin smoothening or hair care? Find answers below.

How long does a keratin treatment last?

With proper sulfate-free aftercare, a professional keratin treatment at our Miami hair salon typically lasts between 3 to 5 months, depending on your natural texture and washing frequency.

Is keratin smoothening safe for color-treated hair?

Yes! In fact, keratin smoothening helps lock in hair color, seals the cuticle layer, and adds a brilliant protective shine to highlighted or color-treated strands.

What is the difference between a traditional hair relaxer and keratin smoothing?

A traditional hair relaxer permanently alters the internal bond structure of your hair, whereas keratin smoothing infuses essential proteins that temporarily relax curls and frizz, wearing off gracefully without harsh demarkation lines.

Can I wash my hair immediately after a keratin smoothing session?

Depending on the specific formula used during your service, you may need to wait 24 to 72 hours before washing or tying up your hair. Our stylists will provide clear, personalized post-treatment care instructions.
